About

I’m completing a Bachelor of Advanced Computing at the University of Sydney, specialising in computational data science. My research focuses on how vision–language models perform under different evaluation conditions and how language guidance affects visual recognition.

I’m interested in computer vision, multimodal learning and model evaluation, and I’m open to related research directions.
